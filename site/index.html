About this page

Hi, I am Daniel.

This is a spare-time project. I like EVs, I like charts, and I like vibe coding after the kids are in bed. Everything on this page comes from public data, mostly Statistics Canada and Transport Canada, pulled by a small script that runs on the 15th of each month and publishes itself.

Please do not use these numbers for planning, buying, investing, or anything that matters. Go to the sources. They are linked below, with the date each one was pulled. Statistics Canada revises data, the series use different definitions, and I am one person checking the output between other things.

What I do promise: every panel says where its numbers come from and what they leave out. No scraping of licensed data. No internal or proprietary information of any kind. This is independent work, not connected to any employer, past or present, or to any data provider.

The code is open source under the MIT licence: github.com/dvaladares/ev-canada-dashboard. If you spot an error, open an issue.
