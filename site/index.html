About this dashboard

  • Independent. A personal project. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any employer, past or present, or by any data provider.
  • Public data only. Every number comes from the sources listed below. Every panel names its source and access date. Nothing here is internal or proprietary.
  • Not advice. This page is for general information. It is not financial, investment, or purchasing advice.
  • Accuracy. Figures are reproduced as published. Statistics Canada revises data, and timing and definitions differ between series. Read the note on each panel before you quote a number.
  • Open source. Code and pipeline: github.com/dvaladares/ev-canada-dashboard (MIT licence). Found an error? Open an issue.
